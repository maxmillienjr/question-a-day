The World at Twenty

Pick the year your mother or father was born. You get one printable page of ten questions about what was happening in the United States when they were 10, 20 and 30: the news, the songs, the prices, the new machines.

Take it on your next visit. Every fact comes from a source you can check, listed under the sheet.

These are facts about the United States.

How to use this

Read the fact out loud, then ask the question under it.

Let them talk, and follow wherever the story goes.

Write down the names, places and dates they mention.

Sources for this sheet
